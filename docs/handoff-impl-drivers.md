## Skipped, and why

- drivers-a 17 (`sweep.fill` everywhere): `FreqSweep.iter`/`at` already
  compute the same grid without a buffer; `fill` would add an omega
  allocation to sp and disto for no behavior change.
- drivers-b 6, the `pss/samples.zig` file: HB and PAC sample differently
  (HB evaluates at an IDFT'd x per sample, PAC runs Newton per sample), so
  the only shared piece is a one-line slot-major copy. The nnz planes were
  done in place in hb.zig and pac.zig. qpss and pnoise keep their dense
  per-sample planes (pnoise is stream C's rewrite; qpss was not in scope).
- sp stays on its dense per-port per-point solve: it never materialized the
  sweep, and a Stream per port would multiply the factorizations.
- Anything output-changing (stream C list) and every dense→sparse or LaneLu
  switch (roundoff) was left alone.
